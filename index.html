📚 Key Vocabulary: The 3Rs

📉 Reduce

To use less of something in order to create less waste and conserve resources.

"We reduce waste by turning off the lights and saving clean water."
🔄 Reuse

To use an item again for the same or a new purpose instead of throwing it away.

"We reuse glass jars to store pencils or dry food."
♻️ Recycle

To collect and transform waste materials into new usable objects in factories.

"We recycle cardboard boxes and plastic to make new notebooks."
